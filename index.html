PFNexus

PFNexus is the ultimate companion for your virtual airline. We provide a suite of powerful, intuitive tools designed to help you manage your community, track operations, and enhance the experience for all your members.

Features

Advanced Moderation

Keep your community safe with automated moderation, custom rules, and a detailed audit log.

Customizable Commands

Create and manage your own commands to tailor the bot to your server's unique needs.

User Analytics

Gain insights into your server's activity with detailed statistics on members, channels, and engagement.
